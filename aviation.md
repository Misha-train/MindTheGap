# Amazing Aviation Facts ✈️

## 1. Why do plane windows have tiny holes?
If you look closely at a passenger plane window, you will see a tiny hole at the bottom. It is called a **bleed hole**. 
* **The Reason:** It regulates air pressure between the inside cabin and the outside sky.
* **Safety:** It ensures that the heavy pressure acts only on the outer pane of glass, keeping the cabin safe.
